03 / HOW WE WORK

Understand first.
Verify together.

AI can assist implementation. People remain responsible for understanding, review and decisions.

  • Start with a real problem. Research alternatives and explain trade-offs.
  • Review the work. Understand the code, its risks and limitations.
  • Verify reproducibly. Pair human judgment with repeatable checks.
  • Share the evidence. Make limitations and decisions clear.
