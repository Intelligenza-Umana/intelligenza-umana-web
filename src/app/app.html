01 / WHY WE EXIST

Tools should amplify human judgment.

We use technology to help people think, build and understand more—not to hide how decisions are made.

Every project begins with a real problem. We make our reasoning visible, check the work regardless of who or what produced it, and say clearly what remains uncertain.

Read the manifesto →

02 / HOW WE WORK

Understand first.
Verify together.

AI output is a starting point. Human understanding and reproducible evidence are what make work ready to share.

  1. Start with a real problem. Define constraints and research alternatives.
  2. Make the reasoning visible. Explain choices, risks and trade-offs.
  3. Review and verify. Understand the code and run repeatable checks.
  4. Be honest about limits. State uncertainty; tests do not prove absolute security.
